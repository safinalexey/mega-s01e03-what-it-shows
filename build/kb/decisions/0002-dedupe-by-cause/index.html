0002 · verify cause before merging reports

owner Aleksei Safin proposed reviewed 2026-11-02 · 1 days ago

Context

The investigation of #4471 shows independent failures merged into a single ticket. Matching subjects does not prove a shared cause. The accepted decision 0001 remains an immutable record of the old choice.

Proposed replacement

Read each report, join its tenant configuration, check the channel and form version, and establish the same cause before merging. Use the subject to find candidates. Missing tenant data or unexplained symptoms require separate investigation. Preserve report IDs and count each once. Use rendered tables and current evidence to check the written policy.
